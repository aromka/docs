A fundraiser is an individual supporter who joins one of your peer-to-peer campaigns and raises money on your organization's behalf. Rather than donating directly themselves, fundraisers create a personal fundraising page — with their own goal, story, and donation link — and share it with their personal network of friends, family, and colleagues. Every gift made through a fundraiser's page rolls up into the campaign's total, so your reach grows with every person who signs up. CrowdChange gives fundraisers the tools they need to promote their page and gives you the admin controls to coach and motivate them throughout the campaign.

## How Fundraisers Work

When you enable peer-to-peer on a campaign, a **Join as a Fundraiser** button appears on your campaign page. A supporter clicks it, creates an account (or logs in if they have one), and their personal fundraising page is generated automatically. The entire registration process takes under two minutes.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Supporter Registers">
    The supporter clicks the join link on your campaign page, enters their name and email address, and creates a CrowdChange account. No admin approval is required — their page goes live immediately.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Fundraiser Customizes Their Page">
    The fundraiser sets a personal goal, writes a short bio or story explaining why they are fundraising, and optionally uploads a profile photo. All customization happens through a simple no-code editor.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Fundraiser Shares Their Page">
    CrowdChange provides each fundraiser with a unique donation link and a downloadable QR code for their personal page. One-click social sharing buttons let them post directly to Facebook, X (Twitter), Instagram, and other platforms.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Donations Flow In">
    Donors who give through the fundraiser's personal page are attributed to that fundraiser. The fundraiser's progress thermometer updates in real time, and both the fundraiser and their donors receive automatic email notifications.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Fundraiser Page Elements

Every personal fundraising page includes the following components out of the box:

<CardGroup cols={2}>
  <Card title="Personal Goal" icon="bullseye">
    Each fundraiser sets their own target amount, independent of the campaign's overall goal. Their progress thermometer tracks how close they are to their personal target, keeping them motivated to keep sharing.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Personal Story" icon="pen-to-square">
    Fundraisers write a short bio explaining their connection to the cause. A compelling personal story is the single biggest driver of peer-to-peer donations, so CrowdChange prompts fundraisers to complete this field during setup.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Progress Thermometer" icon="chart-line">
    A visual fundraising meter displays the fundraiser's current total against their personal goal. The thermometer updates in real time as donations come in, providing instant positive reinforcement.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Personal Donation Link" icon="link">
    Every fundraiser receives a unique, shareable URL for their page (e.g., `yourorg.crowdchange.co/campaign-slug/fundraiser-name`). All donations made through this link are attributed directly to that fundraiser.
  </Card>
</CardGroup>

## Teams

Fundraisers can be grouped into **teams**, allowing colleagues, classrooms, departments, or friend groups to compete and collaborate together. Teams have their own combined fundraising total and appear on a team leaderboard on the main campaign page.

- Any fundraiser can create a new team during registration or join an existing one.
- Teams display a combined progress thermometer and list their individual members with their personal totals.
- You can set a team goal at the campaign level to give teams a shared target to work toward.
- Team captains can send encouragement messages to their team members directly from their fundraiser dashboard.

## Managing Fundraisers as an Admin

Your campaign dashboard gives you full visibility into every fundraiser and the tools to support them.

- **Leaderboards** — View fundraisers and teams ranked by total raised, number of donors, or percent of personal goal reached. Use leaderboards to identify your top performers and those who may need a nudge.
- **Coaching Emails** — Send targeted email messages to specific segments of your fundraisers — for example, everyone who has not yet received their first donation. CrowdChange provides pre-written coaching templates you can customize.
- **Minimum and Suggested Goals** — Set a minimum personal goal that fundraisers must meet to participate and a suggested goal that pre-populates the goal field during registration. Both settings can be adjusted at any time.
- **Fundraiser Records** — Each fundraiser's record shows their total raised, donor count, page views, and registration date, giving you the data you need to understand performance across your entire peer-to-peer network.

<Note>
  Fundraisers receive automated progress notification emails at key milestones (25%, 50%, 75%, and 100% of their personal goal). These emails are sent by CrowdChange on your behalf using your organization's name and branding, so you do not need to manage any outreach manually to keep your fundraisers motivated and on track.
</Note>

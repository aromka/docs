CrowdChange's donation forms are the front door to every [campaign](/concepts/campaigns). They let you capture one-time gifts, recurring monthly pledges, and annual contributions through a single, branded experience that lives on your campaign page or embeds directly into your own website. This page walks you through building a form from scratch, configuring its fields, and making sure every donor receives a timely acknowledgment.

## Creating a Donation Form

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Donation Form settings">
    Navigate to your campaign dashboard and select **Donation Form** from the left sidebar. This opens the form builder where all configuration lives.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose donation types">
    Toggle on the giving frequencies you want to offer: **One-Time**, **Monthly**, and/or **Annual**. You can enable all three and set a default frequency that pre-selects when a donor lands on the form.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Set suggested donation amounts">
    Add up to six suggested amounts — for example, **$25**, **$50**, **$100**, and **$250** — and always include a **Custom** option so donors can enter any amount. You can optionally attach a short impact description to each tier (e.g., *"$50 feeds a family for a week"*).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add custom fields">
    Use the **Custom Fields** panel to capture information beyond the standard donor details. Common additions include an **In Memory Of / In Honor Of** dedication field and an **Employer Name** field to support gift matching workflows.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Brand your form">
    Upload your organization's **logo**, set your **primary and accent colors** using hex codes, and add a **banner image** to the top of the form. All changes preview in real time in the right-hand panel.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Preview and publish">
    Click **Preview** to see exactly what donors will experience on desktop and mobile. When you're satisfied, click **Publish** to make the form live on your campaign page.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Form Fields

Every donation form includes a standard set of fields out of the box. You can mark any field as required or optional, and you can reorder fields by dragging them in the form builder.

<CardGroup cols={3}>
  <Card title="Standard Fields" icon="user">
    First name, last name, email address, phone number, and billing address. Email and name are always required for receipt delivery.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Tribute & Dedication Fields" icon="heart">
    **In Memory Of** and **In Honor Of** fields let donors dedicate their gift. You can optionally notify the honoree or their family via a separate eCard.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Custom Fields" icon="sliders">
    Add free-text, dropdown, checkbox, or date fields for any data your organization needs — employer name, chapter affiliation, dietary preference, and more.
  </Card>
</CardGroup>

<Tip>
  Set one suggested amount as the **highlighted default** — visually emphasizing a mid-range option (such as $100) consistently lifts average gift size. You can mark any amount as the default in the **Suggested Amounts** panel.
</Tip>

## Embedding Your Form

Once your form is published, you can place it anywhere on your own website using a generated embed snippet. Head to [Embeddables](/integrations/embeddables) for the full embed guide, including iframe and JavaScript embed options.

## Donation Acknowledgment

CrowdChange automatically sends a **thank-you email** and a **tax receipt** to every donor immediately after a successful transaction. You can customize the email subject line, body copy, and signature block in **Campaign Settings → Email Templates**. Receipts include the donation amount, date, your organization's EIN, and a statement confirming no goods or services were exchanged (unless you configure otherwise for event tickets or merchandise).

<Note>
  All CrowdChange donation forms are built to meet **WCAG 2.1 AA** accessibility standards. Forms are keyboard-navigable, screen-reader compatible, and use sufficient color contrast so every donor can give with confidence.
</Note>

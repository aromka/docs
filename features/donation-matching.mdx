Few fundraising levers are as effective as a matching gift offer. Donors who see their contribution doubled — or more — feel an immediate, concrete sense of additional impact, and campaigns with active matches consistently outperform those without. CrowdChange makes it straightforward to surface a matching offer to every donor at the moment they give, without requiring any complex payment routing on your end.

## How CrowdChange Matching Works

You define an offline pool of committed funds (for example, a major donor or corporate sponsor has agreed to contribute up to $50,000) and set a multiplier that determines how each dollar donated is matched. CrowdChange tracks how much of the matching pool has been consumed in real time and displays a live matching thermometer on your [campaign](/concepts/campaigns) page. When the pool is exhausted, matching automatically stops — no manual intervention needed.

<Note>
  Matching funds are tracked as an offline pool within CrowdChange. The actual disbursement of matched funds to your organization is handled outside the platform, directly with the matching donor or sponsor.
</Note>

## Setting Up a Match

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Donation Matching for your campaign">
    Navigate to your campaign and select **Donation Matching** from the campaign menu.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Enable matching">
    Click **Enable Matching** to activate the feature for this campaign.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Enter the matching pool amount">
    Enter the total funds your matching donor has committed — for example, **$50,000**. This is the maximum amount that can be matched across all donations.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Set the multiplier">
    Choose the match rate: **1x** (dollar-for-dollar), **1.5x**, **2x**, or a custom value. A 2x multiplier means every $100 donation draws $200 from the matching pool, effectively tripling the donor's impact.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Optionally set a matching period">
    Toggle on **Matching Period** and select a start and end date if you want the match to be active only during a specific window — for example, the final 48 hours of your campaign.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Save and publish">
    Click **Save**. The matching thermometer immediately appears on your campaign page, showing the total pool and how much remains available.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## The Donor Experience

From the moment a donor opens your donation form during an active match, they see clear messaging — **"Your gift will be matched!"** — alongside the current multiplier and remaining pool. This visibility creates a real sense of urgency: donors understand that the matching funds are limited and that acting now maximizes their impact.

## The Matching Thermometer

The thermometer widget on your campaign page displays:

<CardGroup cols={2}>
  <Card title="Total Matching Pool" icon="sack-dollar">
    The full amount committed by your matching donor or sponsor.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Remaining Match Available" icon="chart-simple">
    How much of the pool is still available, updated in real time as donations come in.
  </Card>
</CardGroup>

<Tip>
  Try activating a **2x match** during the final two to four hours of your campaign. The combination of a deadline and doubled impact is one of the most reliable ways to generate a surge of last-minute donations.
</Tip>

## Reporting

The campaign dashboard shows a breakdown of matched versus unmatched donation totals, so you can clearly communicate to your matching sponsor exactly how their funds were deployed. The **Donation Matching** report lists each matched donation, the multiplier applied, and the corresponding draw on the pool — giving you everything you need for post-campaign reporting and stewardship.

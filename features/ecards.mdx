eCards and tribute cards turn a donation into a shareable, personal moment — and that emotional connection drives more giving. Whether you're running a memorial fund where supporters give in someone's honor, a holiday campaign where donors send seasonal greetings alongside their gift, or a year-round store where supporters can buy cards as standalone items, CrowdChange gives you the tools to design beautiful cards, set up a storefront, and automatically deliver them to recipients the moment a gift is made.

## Two Ways to Use Cards

CrowdChange supports two complementary card modes that you can use independently or together:

<CardGroup cols={2}>
  <Card title="Tribute Cards" icon="heart">
    Attached directly to a donation. When a donor gives, they can select a card, write a personal message, and enter the recipient's email. The recipient is notified automatically.
  </Card>
  <Card title="eCard Store" icon="store">
    A standalone branded storefront where supporters can purchase cards — either as an add-on to a donation or as an independent purchase. Ideal for holiday collections and anniversary campaigns.
  </Card>
</CardGroup>

## Creating a Card Design

<Steps>
  <Step title="Start a new card design">
    Go to **Cards** in your organization dashboard and click **New Card Design**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add your artwork">
    Upload your own card artwork (PNG or JPG) or use the built-in designer to compose a card using your brand colors, fonts, and imagery directly in the browser.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Name and categorize the card">
    Enter a card name, a short description, and assign a category (for example, *In Memory*, *Happy Holidays*, or *Thank You*). Categories help donors browse your store quickly.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Set a price">
    Enter a purchase price for store cards, or set the price to **$0** for free tribute cards that are included with any donation.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Configure the recipient email template">
    Customize the email the recipient will receive. You can include your organization's logo, a personal message field for the donor, and a link back to your campaign or website.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Publish to your card store">
    Click **Publish**. The card is immediately available in your store and, if applicable, on your donation forms.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## The eCard Store

Your card store is a branded, shareable page where supporters can browse your full card collection and make purchases. Each card purchase can be linked to a specific campaign so revenue is attributed correctly. Donors who buy a card without making an additional donation are still captured as supporters in your CrowdChange contact records.

<Info>
  You can embed your card store directly on your organization's website using the generated embed code — no additional development work required.
</Info>

## Tribute Cards on Donation Forms

Enable the **Tribute** option on any [donation form](/features/donation-forms) to let donors attach a card to their gift at checkout. When tribute is enabled, donors see a card-selection step during the donation flow where they can:

- Browse available tribute card designs
- Write a personal message to the recipient
- Enter the recipient's name and email address

The tribute attachment is optional — donors who prefer to give without a card can skip the step entirely.

## Recipient Notification

The moment a tribute card is attached to a completed donation, CrowdChange automatically emails the recipient. The email includes the card artwork, the donor's personal message, and — if you choose — a note about the gift amount or the campaign it supports. Recipients don't need a CrowdChange account to view their card.

<Tip>
  Build seasonal card collections — a winter holiday set, a spring campaign series, or an anniversary collection — and rotate them throughout the year. Limited-time card designs create a sense of novelty that encourages repeat donors to give again.
</Tip>

## Reporting

The **Cards** section of your dashboard shows a complete breakdown of card sales by design and category, revenue attributed to each card, and tribute activity linked to donations. Use this data to identify which card designs resonate most with your donors and inform future design investments.

Peer-to-peer fundraising turns your supporters into fundraisers. Instead of a single [campaign](/concepts/campaigns) page collecting all donations, each participant gets their own branded page where they can share their personal story, set a goal, and ask their network to give. Because people donate to people they know and trust, peer-to-peer campaigns consistently outperform traditional donation pages in both reach and total revenue. This page explains how to enable and manage peer-to-peer fundraising in CrowdChange.

## How It Works

<Steps>
  <Step title="Enable peer-to-peer on your campaign">
    Open your campaign dashboard, go to **Campaign Settings → Peer-to-Peer**, and toggle the feature on. You'll be prompted to set a campaign-level fundraising goal and a suggested personal goal that each fundraiser will see when they register.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Supporters register and create personal pages">
    Once enabled, a **Join as a Fundraiser** button appears on your campaign's public page. Supporters click it, complete the registration form, and a personal fundraising page is automatically created for them.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Each fundraiser gets a personal link, QR code, and progress tracker">
    Every fundraiser receives a unique URL and an auto-generated QR code pointing to their personal page. A live progress bar shows how much they've raised against their individual goal in real time.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Supporters share their pages">
    Fundraisers can share their personal link directly from their dashboard using built-in one-click buttons for Facebook, X (Twitter), Instagram, and email — or by copying their QR code to use in printed materials.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Donations are tracked per fundraiser">
    Every donation made through a personal fundraising page is attributed to that fundraiser and rolls up to the campaign total. You can see both the individual and aggregate totals in your admin dashboard at any time.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Fundraiser Registration

The registration flow is fully configurable. You can collect any information you need from participants before their page goes live.

<CardGroup cols={2}>
  <Card title="Custom Registration Form" icon="clipboard-list">
    Add fields for t-shirt size, emergency contact, dietary restrictions, or any other data your event requires. Fields can be marked required or optional.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Registration Fees" icon="credit-card">
    Optionally charge a registration fee to cover event costs. Fees are collected at sign-up and are separate from fundraising proceeds.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Waivers & Acknowledgments" icon="file-signature">
    Include a liability waiver or participation agreement that registrants must check before completing sign-up. Acceptances are logged with a timestamp.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Approval Workflow" icon="circle-check">
    Choose between instant approval or manual review of each registration. Manual review lets you vet participants before their page goes public.
  </Card>
</CardGroup>

## Personal Fundraising Pages

Each fundraiser has full control over their personal page within the boundaries you set.

- **Personal goal** — Fundraisers can set or adjust their own dollar goal. You can set a minimum or a suggested goal in campaign settings.
- **Personal story** — A rich-text editor lets fundraisers write about why this cause matters to them, embed photos, and customize their page headline.
- **Progress display** — A live thermometer graphic updates as donations come in, giving donors an immediate sense of impact.
- **Activity feed** — Recent donors (with optional anonymization) appear on the page, creating social proof and momentum.

## Teams

Supporters can form or join **teams** to compete collectively. Each team gets its own combined leaderboard entry and team page. Team captains can recruit members and send messages to their team from within the platform.

<CardGroup cols={2}>
  <Card title="Team Leaderboard" icon="trophy">
    The campaign leaderboard ranks both individual fundraisers and teams by total amount raised. Leaderboards are public by default and update in real time.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Team Pages" icon="users">
    Every team has its own public page showing team members, a combined progress bar, and a team-specific donation link.
  </Card>
</CardGroup>

## Admin Tools

Your admin dashboard gives you everything you need to coach fundraisers toward their goals.

<CardGroup cols={2}>
  <Card title="Fundraiser Performance Dashboard" icon="chart-bar">
    See each fundraiser's total raised, number of donors, last activity date, and progress against their goal — all in a sortable table.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Coaching Emails" icon="envelope">
    Send targeted emails to fundraiser segments — for example, everyone who has raised less than $100 in the past seven days — directly from your dashboard.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Goal Nudges" icon="bell">
    Configure automated nudge emails that trigger when a fundraiser reaches 25%, 50%, and 75% of their goal, celebrating progress and encouraging the final push.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Leaderboard Controls" icon="list-ol">
    Pin featured fundraisers to the top of the leaderboard, hide participants who opt out of public display, and reset test fundraisers before launch.
  </Card>
</CardGroup>

## Social Sharing

Every personal fundraising page includes auto-generated sharing assets so fundraisers can spread the word with minimal effort.

- **One-click social buttons** for Facebook, X, Instagram, LinkedIn, and email
- **Unique QR code** suitable for print flyers, business cards, or digital slides
- **Pre-written share copy** that fundraisers can edit before posting

<Tip>
  Set a **suggested personal goal** in your campaign settings — something achievable but motivating, like $500. Fundraisers who see a concrete target when they register raise significantly more than those who start with a blank goal field.
</Tip>

Many fundraising efforts rely on commitments made before payment is collected — think walk-a-thon sponsors who pledge per mile, major gift officers securing verbal commitments, or phone-a-thon volunteers logging pledges in real time. CrowdChange's pledging tools let you capture every commitment the moment it's made, keep your pipeline organized, and give donors a frictionless path to fulfilling their gift online on their own schedule.

## What Is a Pledge?

A pledge is a donor's commitment to give a specific amount, recorded before payment is collected. Pledges live alongside your online donations in the [campaign](/concepts/campaigns) dashboard, so you always have a complete picture of your fundraising progress — not just what has already cleared, but everything that's on the way.

## Recording a Pledge

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open your campaign's Pledges tab">
    Navigate to your campaign and select the **Pledges** tab from the campaign menu.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add a new pledge">
    Click **Add Pledge** and fill in the donor's details: name, email address, pledge amount, and any internal notes (for example, the solicitor's name or the event where the pledge was made).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Save the pledge">
    Click **Save**. The pledge immediately appears in your pending pledges list and its amount is reflected in your campaign totals.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Send a fulfillment link">
    Select the pledge and click **Send Fulfillment Link**. CrowdChange emails the donor a unique link to a pre-filled donation form where they can complete their gift by credit card whenever they're ready.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Fulfillment Links

Each pledge generates a unique, one-time fulfillment link tied to that specific donor and amount. When the donor clicks the link they land on a pre-filled donation form — their name, email, and pledge amount are already populated. They simply enter their payment details and submit. No account creation required.

## Pledge Statuses

CrowdChange tracks every pledge through its lifecycle:

<CardGroup cols={3}>
  <Card title="Pending" icon="clock">
    The pledge has been recorded but the donor has not yet completed payment.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Fulfilled" icon="circle-check">
    The donor has clicked their fulfillment link and successfully completed their donation.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Cancelled" icon="circle-xmark">
    The pledge has been voided and is excluded from campaign totals.
  </Card>
</CardGroup>

## Bulk Import via CSV

Running a large pledge drive? Instead of entering pledges one by one, you can import them in bulk using a CSV file. Download the pledge import template from the **Pledges** tab, fill in your donor data, and upload the file. All imported pledges are created in **Pending** status and fulfillment links can be dispatched in a single batch send.

## Automated Reminder Emails

Donors get busy. CrowdChange can automatically send reminder emails to anyone who hasn't fulfilled their pledge after a set number of days. Configure the reminder schedule — for example, three days after the pledge is recorded, then again at seven days — and CrowdChange handles the follow-up for you.

<Tip>
  Pledging works especially well alongside gala events and phone campaigns. Record pledges at the event or during calls, then trigger a batch fulfillment-link send the following morning while donor enthusiasm is still high.
</Tip>

## Reporting

Pledge totals appear in your campaign dashboard side by side with online donation totals, giving you an accurate view of total fundraising at a glance. The **Pledges** report breaks down counts and amounts by status — pending, fulfilled, and cancelled — so you can forecast expected revenue and prioritize outreach to donors who still have outstanding commitments.

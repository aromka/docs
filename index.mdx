## Get started in three steps

<Steps>
  <Step title="Create your account">
    Visit [crowdchange.co](https://crowdchange.co) and request a demo or sign up. Your account includes access to the campaign builder, reporting dashboard, and all platform features.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Build your first campaign">
    Use the no-code campaign builder to configure your donation form, set a fundraising goal, and publish your campaign. No developer needed.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Connect your tools">
    Embed donation forms on your website, connect webhooks to your CRM, or use the REST API to pull donation data into your own systems.
  </Step>
</Steps>

CrowdChange's embeddable donation forms let you keep donors on your own website while still powering the entire giving experience through CrowdChange. Instead of redirecting supporters to a separate page, you can drop a fully functional, branded donation form directly into your site — maintaining trust, reducing drop-off, and making it easy for visitors to give without ever leaving your content.

## Getting Your Embed Code

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open your campaign's Donation Form">
    From your CrowdChange dashboard, navigate to your campaign and select the **Donation Form** tab.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Click Embed in the top right">
    Look for the **Embed** button in the upper-right corner of the Donation Form editor.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose your embed type">
    Select either **Inline** (renders the form directly in your page) or **Popup** (adds a floating donate button that opens the form in a modal).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Copy the generated code snippet">
    CrowdChange generates a ready-to-use code snippet. Click **Copy** to copy it to your clipboard.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Paste it into your website's HTML">
    Open your website editor or CMS, find the location where you want the form to appear, and paste the snippet into the HTML source.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Embed Types

CrowdChange offers two embed formats so you can choose what fits your site's layout best.

**Inline embed** renders the full donation form inside an `<iframe>` directly within your page layout. This is the best choice for a dedicated "Donate" page where the form is the main focus.

**Popup embed** adds a floating donate button anywhere on your page. When a visitor clicks it, the donation form opens in a modal overlay. This works well in sidebars, sticky headers, or anywhere you want a persistent call-to-action without taking up layout space.

### Inline Embed Code

```html
<!-- CrowdChange Inline Donation Form -->
<iframe
  src="https://give.crowdchange.co/embed/YOUR_CAMPAIGN_ID"
  width="100%"
  height="600"
  frameborder="0"
  scrolling="no"
  allowtransparency="true">
</iframe>
```

### Popup Embed Code

```html
<!-- CrowdChange Popup Donation Button -->
<script
  src="https://give.crowdchange.co/embed.js"
  data-campaign="YOUR_CAMPAIGN_ID"
  data-button-label="Donate Now"
  data-button-color="#2563EB">
</script>
```

## Customization Options

You can adjust the following attributes directly in the embed code to match your site's branding:

<CardGroup cols={3}>
  <Card title="Button Label" icon="tag">
    Change the `data-button-label` attribute to any call-to-action text, such as `"Give Today"` or `"Support Us"`.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Button Color" icon="palette">
    Set `data-button-color` to any hex color code to match your organization's brand palette.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Form Height" icon="arrow-up-down">
    Adjust the `height` attribute on the `<iframe>` to control how tall the inline form appears on your page.
  </Card>
</CardGroup>

## Live Updates

One of the biggest advantages of CrowdChange embeds is that they stay in sync automatically. If you update your donation form in CrowdChange — changing the suggested amounts, adding a cover fees option, or updating the branding — those changes are reflected in your embedded form instantly. You do not need to update or re-paste the embed code on your website.

<Note>
  The embed code is unique to each campaign. If you create a new campaign, you will need to generate and paste a new embed code for that campaign. The old embed will continue to point to the original campaign.
</Note>

<Tip>
  Before adding the embed to your live website, test it in a staging or development environment first. This lets you confirm the form loads correctly, fits your layout, and that donations flow through to your CrowdChange dashboard as expected.
</Tip>

CrowdChange is an end-to-end digital fundraising platform built specifically for nonprofits. These docs walk you through everything you need — from launching your first campaign to integrating with your own applications via the REST API.

## What is CrowdChange?

CrowdChange gives your organization a single place to create fundraising campaigns, collect donations, manage events, and measure impact. Instead of stitching together separate tools for forms, ticketing, peer-to-peer fundraising, and reporting, you get every capability in one platform that works together out of the box.

Whether you are running a one-day giving day, a multi-location gala, or an always-on donation page, CrowdChange handles the complexity so your team can focus on your mission.

## Platform Features at a Glance

CrowdChange brings together every tool a modern nonprofit fundraising program needs:

- **No-code campaign builder** — create, customize, and publish campaigns without writing any code.
- **Embeddable donation forms** — drop a donation form onto any webpage with a single auto-generated snippet.
- **Peer-to-peer fundraising** — let supporters create personal fundraising pages and rally their own networks.
- **Event ticketing with QR code verification** — sell tickets, manage check-in, and scan attendees at the door.
- **eCard store and tribute card designer** — donors can send personalized tribute eCards as part of their gift.
- **Pledging and offline donation tracking** — record pledges and cash or check gifts alongside online donations.
- **Donation matching with configurable multipliers** — set up matching gift programs with custom match ratios.
- **Real-time reporting and analytics** — track totals, donor counts, conversion rates, and traffic sources live.
- **Blueprinting for multi-location events** — create master campaign templates that chapters and regions inherit automatically.

## Who is CrowdChange for?

CrowdChange is designed for mission-driven organizations of every size and type:

- **Nonprofits and charities** that need a reliable, donor-friendly way to accept online gifts and run recurring giving programs.
- **Universities and alumni associations** running annual funds, scholarship drives, and day-of-giving events across multiple schools or departments.
- **Faith-based organizations** managing congregation giving, capital campaigns, and special appeals.
- **Foundations and community groups** coordinating peer-to-peer fundraising walks, runs, and challenge events.
- **Multi-location organizations** that need master templates and blueprinting to spin up consistent campaigns across chapters or regions.

If your organization raises money online and wants to do it more effectively, CrowdChange was built for you.

This guide walks you through the fastest path from sign-up to a live fundraising campaign. By the end, you will have a published campaign with a donation form, a shareable link, and your first donation recorded in the reporting dashboard — all without writing a single line of code.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Create your account">
    Head to [crowdchange.co](https://crowdchange.co) and click **Request a Demo** to connect with the CrowdChange team. During the demo your account will be provisioned and your organization profile set up — including your nonprofit's name, logo, and primary contact details.

    Once your account is active, you will receive a welcome email with a link to log in to your dashboard for the first time.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Set up your first campaign">
    From your dashboard, click **New Campaign** to open the no-code campaign builder.

    1. Give your campaign a name and a compelling description.
    2. Set a fundraising **goal amount** and a campaign **end date** (or leave it open-ended for always-on campaigns).
    3. Choose which **donation types** to enable:
       - **One-time** — a single gift of any amount
       - **Monthly** — a recurring gift billed every month
       - **Annual** — a recurring gift billed once per year

    You can mix and match donation types on a single campaign. Enabling monthly and annual options is one of the fastest ways to grow sustainable, predictable revenue for your organization.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Add and customize a donation form">
    Inside the campaign builder, navigate to the **Donation Form** tab. From here you can:

    - Set **suggested amounts** (e.g. $25, $50, $100, $250) and enable a custom amount field.
    - Add or remove **form fields** such as phone number, employer, or a personal dedication message.
    - Apply your organization's **branding** — colors, fonts, and logo — so the form feels native to your website.
    - Enable **donation matching** and configure a multiplier if your organization has a matching gift program (for example, a 2× match doubles every donor's gift).

    Every change is reflected instantly in the live preview panel on the right.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Publish and share your campaign">
    When you are happy with your campaign, click **Publish**. CrowdChange immediately generates three ways to share it:

    - **Shareable link** — a hosted campaign page you can send in emails, text messages, or social posts.
    - **QR code** — download a print-ready QR code to use on flyers, signage, or event materials.
    - **Embed code** — a short HTML snippet you can paste into any webpage or CMS to embed the donation form directly on your site.

    Use the **Social Sharing** panel to pre-compose posts for Facebook, X (Twitter), and LinkedIn.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Accept your first donation">
    With your campaign live, make a small test donation through your own campaign page to confirm the end-to-end flow:

    1. Open your shareable campaign link.
    2. Select a donation amount and fill in the donor details.
    3. Complete the checkout using a real or test payment method.

    You will receive a donation confirmation email, and the gift will appear in your dashboard immediately. This is also a good moment to verify your donation receipt template looks exactly how you want it.
  </Step>

  <Step title="View results in the reporting dashboard">
    Navigate to **Reports** in the left-hand sidebar to see your campaign's real-time analytics:

    - **Total raised** vs. your goal, displayed as a live progress bar.
    - **Donor count**, average gift size, and conversion rate.
    - **Donation timeline** showing spikes in activity.
    - **Breakdown by donation type** (one-time vs. recurring) and by traffic source.

    All reports can be exported to CSV for use in your CRM or finance tools.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Tip>
  If your organization runs the same type of event across multiple chapters, regions, or campus locations, use **Blueprinting** to create a master campaign template. Every location can then spin up their own campaign in seconds while inheriting your approved branding, donation amounts, and settings. Find out more in the [Campaigns](/concepts/campaigns) guide.
</Tip>

## What to explore next

Now that your first campaign is live, dig deeper into the features that will have the biggest impact on your fundraising:

- [Embeddables](/integrations/embeddables) — Embed donation forms and campaign widgets directly on your website or donor portal.
- [Peer-to-Peer Fundraising](/features/peer-to-peer) — Let your supporters create personal fundraising pages and rally their own networks on your behalf.
- [Ticketing](/features/ticketing) — Sell event tickets, distribute QR codes, and verify attendees at check-in.
- [API Reference](/api-reference/overview) — Automate campaign management and sync donor data with your CRM or data warehouse using the CrowdChange REST API.
